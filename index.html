❓ Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

What is the minimum order quantity? +
For retail orders, the minimum is 5 kg per variety. For wholesale/bulk pricing, the minimum is 25 kg. We also accept custom quantities for large export orders — just WhatsApp us.
Do you ship pan-India? +
Yes! We deliver across all of India using trusted courier partners like DTDC, BlueDart, and India Post. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days. Bulk orders (25 kg+) are dispatched within 2–3 working days.
Are your Kissmiss naturally processed without preservatives? +
Yes — all our raisins are naturally sun-dried without artificial additives, sulphur dioxide, or preservatives. Our Organic Kissmiss is grown without synthetic pesticides and is tested for purity.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), and cash on delivery for local Nashik pickup. For new customers, we require 100% advance. Repeat buyers may avail credit terms.
Can I visit the farm directly? +
Absolutely! We welcome buyers to visit our farm at Kede, Ugaon, Niphad, Nashik. Please call or WhatsApp us before visiting so we can arrange a proper welcome and farm tour for you.
Do you offer custom packaging for gifts or retail brands? +
Yes, we offer custom packaging for exporters, gift packers, and retail brands. This includes branded pouches, vacuum-sealed packs, and bulk sacks. WhatsApp us with your requirements for a custom quote.
What is the shelf life of your Kissmiss? +
Properly stored in a cool, dry place, our raisins have a shelf life of 12–18 months. Vacuum-sealed packs last longer. Avoid moisture and direct sunlight for best results.
Do you supply to export markets? +
Yes, we supply to exporters and overseas buyers. Our Jumbo and Organic Kissmiss are popular for export packaging. Contact us for export documentation, phytosanitary certificates, and custom packing requirements.
